What I Build

Playable prototypes and game systems, with the assets to go with them.

  • Character controllers and movement that feel responsive on keyboard and gamepad
  • Physics interactions: pushing, carrying, stacking
  • Game-ready props with clean topology, LODs and correct scale

How I Approach It

Short loops between building and testing.

  • Get a mechanic playable first, polish later
  • Playtest often and tune by feel, not by spreadsheet
  • Keep the Blender-to-engine pipeline consistent so assets drop in cleanly
